Before you begin

You already own the raw material.

Every trip you have taken holds a handful of moments, a few people, and some details that only you noticed. This worksheet helps you find them, check them, and shape them into a month of posts that sound like you.

Pick one trip. Write quickly. You can polish later.

Your month

Four weeks, one story.

Write the first line you would post for each week. Everything else follows from it.

WeekFocus and formatsMy first line
1The moment.
A short video, a photo with a story caption, or a story sequence.		 
 
2The person.
A portrait with a caption naming their gift (with permission), or a short clip of them speaking.		 
 
3What to know before you go.
A carousel or post that answers the questions your clients ask.		 
 
4The invitation.
Your story in one paragraph, with an invitation to talk about a trip.		 
 

Four checks before you post

Permission. Names, faces, and photos of other people.
Accuracy. Every fact traces back to Step 5.
Gratitude. Credit the people and places that made it possible.
One invitation. A single, clear next step for the reader.
