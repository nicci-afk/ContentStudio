The five days

Built for advisors.

Day 1
Feb 8

Arrival and orientation

A private transfer from the Cancún airport to the villa in Akumal. A welcome dinner prepared by the private chef opens the first education session, led by Nathan Riddle and Kha Ly.

Day 2
Feb 9

Sail the Caribbean

A private catamaran charter with a full crew and a personal English-speaking host, four hours along the INAH Reef near Puerto Aventuras.

Day 3
Feb 10

Mayan jungle expedition

Four cenotes, zip lines through the jungle canopy, and a Mayan village, guided by Living Dreams Mexico.

Day 4
Feb 11

Dream Muyil

The Sian Ka'an Biosphere. Freshwater channels through a Mayan archaeological zone, floating between lagoons ringed by jungle.

Day 5
Feb 12

Departure

Your private transfer back to the Cancún airport is arranged and handled.
